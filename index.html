Before We Start

Simple answers to the questions clients usually ask before beginning a project.

Ask a Question

1.What do you need from me to get started?

Your goal, preferred features, examples you like, available content, and any deadline or budget constraints. If the scope is unclear, I can help define it.

2.How long will my project take?

Timing depends on scope and integrations. After reviewing the requirements, I provide a realistic schedule with clear milestones before development begins.

3.Will I receive updates and revisions?

Yes. I share progress at agreed milestones, collect focused feedback, and complete the revisions included in the agreed scope.

4.Can you deploy and support the finished product?

Yes. I can support deployment, configuration, testing, bug fixes, performance improvements, and ongoing maintenance based on your needs.

5.How do pricing and confidentiality work?

Pricing is based on scope, complexity, and timeline. I confirm deliverables and cost before work starts and can work under an NDA when required.
